Text that is wired to the mains

The sign is your own heading. Nothing is measured, split or duplicated: the tube is the host's live text, the glow is a stack of blurred copies of it, and the pool of light underneath is two blend-mode layers that light the page rather than sit on it.

It lights what it sits on

The spill is the difference between a sign in a room and a sign floating in front of one. Turn spill down to zero and the photograph goes back to how it was shot; turn it up and the light lands on the pavement.

Usage

import { createNeonSign } from './effects/neon-sign';

const sign = createNeonSign(document.querySelector('h1'), {
  color: '#ff2e63',
  glowRadius: 18,
  spill: 0.55,
  flicker: 0.3,
});

sign.setOptions({ lit: false }); // cold grey glass, no glow
sign.destroy();

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the sign stays lit and glowing and stops buzzing — the treatment is the point, the flicker is the motion. Toggle the preference in system settings and this page follows without a reload.
